Intentional differences and limits
  • Offers around a selected area replaces “Delivering to” and delivery estimates. The compact store-directory shortcut replaces the cart count.
  • All offers / In-store / Online use supported fulfillment. Ending soon, Categories and Bank Offers replace unsupported ranking, free-delivery and BOGO filters.
  • Featured offers replaces an unverified “Top” ranking. Actual bank names and card conditions replace registered-card eligibility claims.
  • View offer opens the existing detail/action flow. No unsupported direct redemption, claimed counts, scarcity or capacity progress.
  • Existing controlled food photography substitutes for the exact pizza, sushi and waffle reference images. No reference photos were added to app assets.
  • Platform typography, 44–48px touch targets, explicit restrictions and fixture labels can make cards taller. Dark mode and enlarged/localized text adapt rather than shrinking type.
